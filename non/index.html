What is Non-Renewable Energy?

Non-renewable energy comes from things that exist in limited amounts and cannot be remade. Once these resources are burnt, they are gone for good. They are made of fossil fuels (coal, oil, and natural gas) and radioactive materials like uranium.

Types of Non-Renewable Energy

  • 🪨 Coal A fossilized rock that is burned to generate electricity and heat.
  • 🛢️ Oil (Petroleum) A liquid fossil fuel extracted from the earth, heavily refined into gasoline, diesel, and jet fuel to power transportation.
  • 💨 Natural Gas A gas trapped in a rock formation, mostly made of methane, used widely for home heating and cooking.
  • ⚛️ Nuclear Energy Uses radioactive elements, mostly uranium, which are mined and processed to generate massive amounts of electricity through nuclear fission.

How They Formed

Fossil fuels took hundreds of millions of years to form. They are the preserved, prehistoric plants and animals that were thougt to be squashed underground by heat and pressure.

Pros and Cons

👍 Advantages

They are pretty cheap to get and make more power than renewable energy.
